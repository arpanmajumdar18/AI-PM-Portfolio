## 📌 Executive Summary
**[View Detailed Executive Summary](https://celestial-physician-a13.notion.site/ElevenLabs-Growth-Project-Executive-Summary-2f77aa893b948080a6d0f832394072f1?source=copy_link)**


**Problem**  
ElevenLabs needed a scalable, enterprise‑ready go‑to‑market motion for its AI Voice Agents Platform. The challenge: serve **two fundamentally different audiences** (startups seeking affordable scale vs. enterprises requiring security and transformation) without diluting brand or wasting resources.

**Solution**  
A **segmented, hypothesis‑driven GTM strategy**:

- **Two dedicated landing pages** tailored to each ICP, built and launched on Lovable.
- **Full marketing automation design** (HubSpot‑first) with lead scoring, nurture sequences, and integration architecture.
- **Enterprise‑grade content assets** – ROI whitepaper, competitive battle cards, and a founder‑focused blog post.
- **SEO & demand capture plan** targeting high‑intent keywords for each segment.
- **30‑day experiment blueprint** with a $10K budget to validate efficiency and CAC.

**Outcome**  
A complete, executable playbook ready for pilot. Projected to increase lead volume by **40%** and improve sales qualification efficiency by **25%** over a generic approach.

## 🧠 Strategic Approach

| Principle | Application in This Project |
|----------|-----------------------------|
